Research notes

For 13.60, the most credible development foundation is the current PS5 Payload SDK. A payload being buildable for 13.60 does not mean a public browser-to-kernel jailbreak exists for 13.60.

This host intentionally does not copy or auto-run unverified kernel UAF code. Once an independently verified 13.60 entrypoint is public, it can be added as a separate, opt-in module.
